INGRAMMICRO Intake Agent PILOT

Dynamics 365 and Outlook opportunities, checked against the AWS funding framework.

The agent fetches opportunities from Dynamics and from your Outlook mailbox into one list. Opportunities found in both are combined, and the Data source column shows where each one came from. It works out segment, workload and AWS spend (ARR = MRR × 12) and applies the AWS Funding Programs Decision Framework. Check one opportunity or the whole pipeline at once.
